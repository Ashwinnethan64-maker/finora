### Sub-Brand Specifics & Mandates:

1. **Finora Advisory (Financial & Corporate Consulting):**
   * *Disciplines:* 3-Statement financial modeling, SME working capital compression, bank debt syndication, corporate valuation, and Section 54F ESOP tax strategy.
   * *Audience:* Founders, mid-market industrial promoters, and HNIs.

2. **Finora Intelligence (Financial Analytics & Dashboards):**
   * *Capabilities:* Real-time KPI telemetry, SKU contribution margins (CM1/CM2), debtor aging tracking, and cohort retention engines.

3. **Finora Research (Equity & Market Research):**
   * *Publications:* Sectoral EV/ARR valuation multiples, Indian macro policy dispatches, and downloadable institutional financial model templates (.xlsx).

4. **Finora OS (Financial Management SaaS):**
   * *Software:* The autonomous financial operating system featuring 13-week rolling cash forecasts, scenario planners, and encrypted data room integrations.

5. **Finora Insights (Articles, Reports & Education):**
   * *Flagship Series:* The recurring **"India SME Financial Health Index"**, case teardowns, and financial literacy playbooks for capital allocators.

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual Design Tokens

### Color Hierarchy
* **Primary Deep Charcoal (Canvas):** `#080B11` / `hsl(222, 36%, 5%)`
* **Surface Secondary (Cards):** `#0F1420` / `hsl(220, 27%, 9%)`
* **Elevated Surface (Panels):** `#171F30` / `hsl(220, 25%, 14%)`
* **Text Off-White (Primary Typography):** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
* **Text Secondary (Sub-headlines):** `#A1B2C6` (WCAG AAA contrast)
* **Single Distinctive Accent (Sovereign Gold):** `#D4AF37` / `#E5C058` (Authority, wealth, and analytical precision)

### Typography Hierarchy
* **Brand & Display Headings:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Weight: 700 / 800) — Modern, authoritative geometric sans-serif.
* **Body & Long-Form Reading:** *Inter* (Weight: 400 / 500 / 600) — Neutral, crystal-clear readability.
* **Financial Numerals & Tables:** *JetBrains Mono* (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) — Monospace alignment for balance sheets.

### Logo & Symbol Direction
* **Custom "F" Flow Symbol:** Geometric interlocking dynamic strokes representing financial flow and structural stability, avoiding generic ₹ rupee symbols or stock bar charts.
