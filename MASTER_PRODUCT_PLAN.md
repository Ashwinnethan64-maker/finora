## 1. Primary & Secondary ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles)

### 🎯 Primary ICPs (Revenue Drivers)

#### ICP-A: The High-Growth Startup Founder / Seed-to-Series B Operator
* **Demographics:** Age 26–42, Bengaluru/NCR/Mumbai/Pune/Hyderabad. Leading tech, D2C, or B2B platforms ($500K to $10M ARR / ₹4 Cr to ₹80 Cr).
* **Persona Attributes:** Product-first or sales-first background; lacks institutional finance/CFO depth. Under intense board scrutiny on burn rate, unit economics (LTV/CAC, cohort gross margin), and runway extensions.
* **Core Trigger:** Preparing for a fundraising round, term-sheet due diligence, investor reporting chaos, or sudden cashflow bleed.

#### ICP-B: The Modernizing Mid-Market SME Owner / Industrial Promoter
* **Demographics:** Age 35–58, Tier-1 & Tier-2 industrial hubs (Ahmedabad, Surat, Coimbatore, Ludhiana, Chennai). Turnover ₹15 Cr – ₹250 Cr.
* **Persona Attributes:** Running on legacy Tally workflows and fragmented CA advice. Has high revenue but low margin visibility, delayed receivables (working capital gridlock), and informal banking arrangements.
* **Core Trigger:** Working capital squeeze, generational transition to digital operations, debt syndication/refinancing needs, or tax restructuring.

#### ICP-C: HNI / C-Suite Executive / Multi-Asset Wealth Creator
* **Demographics:** Age 32–52, Top metros, earning ₹75L+ p.a. or holding high-value unlisted ESOPs/equity stakes.
* **Persona Attributes:** Overwhelmed by generic RM/bank distributor advice pushing ULIPs and high-commission products. Requires objective, un-conflicted asset allocation, ESOP tax optimization, and estate/capital gains planning.
* **Core Trigger:** ESOP liquidity event, company exit, inheritance/capital gain deployment, or cross-border asset structuring.

### 🎓 Secondary ICP (Ecosystem & Talent Flywheel)

#### ICP-D: The Finance Professional / Aspiring Equity Analyst
* **Demographics:** Tier-1/Tier-2 college grads, CA/CFA/FRM aspirants, junior investment banking/PE/VC associates.
* **Persona Attributes:** Frustrated by theoretical academic courses; needs real-world dynamic 3-statement models, DCF/LBO templates, deal comps, and equity research tear-sheets.
* **Strategic Role:** Consumes research notes, uses interactive financial calculators, virally shares data visualizations, and acts as top-of-funnel advocacy agents inside their organizations.

## 12. Legal, Ethical & Regulatory Considerations (India Compliance)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To operate cleanly without regulatory friction in India, the platform architecture strictly separates **Corporate Finance Advisory / Financial Modeling** from regulated **Investment Advisory / Securities Brokerage**.

1. **SEBI Regulations (Investment Advisers Regulations, 2013 & Research Analyst Regulations, 2014):**
   * Clear disclaimers across all research articles: *"Published materials are for educational and financial intelligence purposes only. They do not constitute personalized investment advice or buy/sell recommendations for public securities."*
   * If stock research is published, maintain factual financial teardowns or obtain/partner with a SEBI-registered Research Analyst (RA).
2. **ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India) Regulations:**
   * If the founder is a practicing CA, adhere to ICAI advertising guidelines (avoid superlatives, adhere to Council guidelines on website hosting and firm description).
   * Operate under a distinct corporate consultancy structure (Private Limited / LLP) for management consulting, SaaS, and modeling services.
3. **Data Protection & Privacy (DPDP Act 2023):**
   * Strict consent collection for financial data uploaded to diagnostic tools.
   * End-to-end encryption for uploaded P&L, balance sheets, and cap tables.
   * Comprehensive Privacy Policy and Data Retention disclosures.
